01. Overview

This Privacy Notice applies to the public informational website for iMatchOJT: An AI-Powered OJT Placement, Monitoring, and Document Compliance System for PUP Santa Rosa Campus.

The website introduces the capstone project, explains its features and workflows, and presents the student researchers involved in its development.

This website is separate from the main iMatchOJT application. It does not provide account registration, sign-in, internship applications, or OJT document submission.

02. Information processed

Visitors can browse the informational website without providing their name, email address, or student information through an account or application form.

When the website is accessed online, the hosting provider or related technical services may process information such as:

  • IP address and network connection details.
  • Browser and device information.
  • Pages requested and time of access.
  • Technical error and security records.

The exact information processed depends on the hosting configuration and services enabled for this website.

The creators section may also display approved names, roles, and photographs of project team members.
