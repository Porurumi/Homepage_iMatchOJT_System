01 / THE IDEA

Built for the things that really matter in OJT.

Finding a company is only one part of the journey. Students also need to handle applications, documents, OJT hours, and updates. At the same time, companies and faculty have their own responsibilities.

iMatchOJT brings those tasks together, making the process easier to follow from the first application to the final requirement.

04 / HOW IT WORKS

One system. Four perspectives.

Choose a role to learn how iMatchOJT supports each part of the OJT process.

THE STUDENT JOURNEY

From first search to final requirement.

  1. Complete your profileAdd your interests, skills, and preferred roles.
  2. Discover and applyReview AI recommendations and choose where to apply.
  3. Confirm a placementReview offers and wait for faculty placement setup.
  4. Monitor and completeSubmit documents, DTRs, and required evaluations.
THE COMPANY JOURNEY

Connect with emerging talent.

  1. Join through invitationRegister through an authorized campus invitation.
  2. Post internshipsShare available positions and requirements.
  3. Review applicationsEvaluate students, arrange interviews, and offer slots.
  4. Support traineesCoordinate with faculty and provide evaluations.
THE FACULTY JOURNEY

Give every student clearer guidance.

  1. Choose your assigned classFocus on one active class at a time.
  2. Verify placementsSet up confirmed OJT placements and required hours.
  3. Review documentsCheck DTR submissions and other requirements.
  4. Follow progressReview students' training updates and evaluations.
THE FACULTY HEAD JOURNEY

See the bigger OJT picture.

  1. Manage invitationsCoordinate access for faculty and partner companies.
  2. Oversee assignmentsSupport faculty and class responsibilities.
  3. Monitor partnershipsManage company records and agreement-related tasks.
  4. Track overall statusFollow placement progress and compliance.

AI recommends. People decide. iMatchOJT suggests possible internship matches, but students, companies, and faculty still handle the actual placement process.
